## Banned list

<!-- Phrases, tics and clichés never to use. Starter list of common offenders; edit freely. -->

- "let out a breath (s)he didn't know (s)he was holding"
- "a testament to"
- "tapestry" (figurative)
- "couldn't help but"
- "a mix(ture) of X and Y" for emotions
- "something shifted" / "something in her broke"
- "the weight of" + abstract noun
- "eyes that held" + emotion
- summarising a scene's emotional meaning in its final line
- rhetorical triplets as a habit ("X. Y. Z.")
- "Little did she know"
